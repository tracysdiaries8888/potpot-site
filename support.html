Potpot Support

Need help with Potpot? We’re happy to help.

Before you contact us

  • If an App Store purchase is missing, try Settings → Restore Purchases.
  • If Screen Time blocking behaves unexpectedly, finish or give up the active cook, reopen Potpot, and check that Screen Time permission is still enabled.
  • If an email sign-in code does not arrive, wait a moment, check spam, and request a new code.

Contact

Email: support@potpotfocus.com

Before launch: make sure this address is configured to receive mail. Until then, use your existing support inbox while testing.

Account deletion

You can delete your Potpot account directly in Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your Potpot account and cloud-synced Potpot data. App Store subscriptions are managed separately by Apple.
